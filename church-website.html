Detailed Ministry Breakdown

Adult Sanctuary, Youth Church, and Teenagers Church.

1. The Main Sanctuary (Adult Church)

Identity: The spiritual epicenter of RCBC (Main Campus), serving as a training hub for future global pastors, theologians, and Christ's ambassadors.

Core Focus: Scriptural depth, intensive prayer, classical and contemporary worship, and doctrinal integrity.

Timings: Sunday Celebration Service (8:00 AM), Tuesday Digging Deep (6:00 PM), Thursday Faith Clinic (6:00 PM).

2. The Youth Church

Identity: High-energy, contemporary expressions ministry tailoring word and worship to undergraduates, young professionals, and campus scholars.

Core Focus: Purpose discovery, career excellence, spiritual fire, and community building.

Section Idea ("Ignite & Deploy"): "Arise and Shine" / "Shift Gears", creative arts, alternative music, and vocational seminars. Sundays 8:00 AM.

3. The Teenagers Church

Identity: Vibrant, nurturing, and safe environment designed specifically for teenagers to cross into deep relationships with Christ.

Core Focus: Bible quizzes, discussions on identity/peer pressure, career conferences, and multimedia workshops.

Section Idea ("The Overflow"): Designated overflow wing, interactive Bible study games, film shows, and mentorship panels. Sundays 8:00 AM.
